A little clarity.

Undertone is an independent Spotify lyrics companion. Music plays through Spotify; Undertone displays lyrics and sends the playback commands you choose.

Your Spotify connection

Sign-in goes directly to Spotify using authorization code with PKCE. The page requests access to your playlists, playback state and playback controls. Spotify’s standard public profile access may also appear on its consent screen. No client secret or Spotify password is collected by Undertone.

Access and refresh tokens stay in this browser tab’s session storage. Disconnect removes them. Closing the tab normally ends that session; some browsers can restore sessions. You can revoke access at any time in your Spotify account’s Apps settings. Account tokens are never sent to GitHub or LRCLIB by this app.

Lyrics and local preferences

The current song title, artist, album and duration are sent to LRCLIB to find lyrics. Matched lyrics, imported LRC files and appearance choices are cached in your browser’s local storage. Imported files stay on your device. Clearing this site’s browser data removes those saved preferences and lyrics.

Hosting and artwork

GitHub Pages serves the public application files. Your browser loads album and playlist artwork from Spotify’s image servers. These providers receive normal network information such as your IP address. The app includes no analytics or advertising scripts. Its service worker caches public interface files for offline use, never Spotify API responses or sign-in tokens.

Availability

Spotify playback controls require Premium. Development-mode Spotify apps are limited to their owner and allowed users. Spotify currently restricts playlist contents to playlists you own or collaborate on. Volume control depends on the active device; use physical volume buttons when it is unsupported. On phones, the browser must remain open for live lyric updates.
